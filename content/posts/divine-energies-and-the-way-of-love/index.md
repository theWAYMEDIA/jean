## An Exploration of Wayist Understanding

*by Jean Prieur du Plessis*

In an age when many are turning away from traditional religious structures, seeking a more authentic and direct connection with divine reality, the Wayist understanding offers a profound alternative. This collection explores how the Lord’s teaching transformed humanity’s relationship with heaven, replacing fear with love, judgment with growth, and religious control with direct spiritual access.

Beginning with the ancient dharani that speaks of both yang and yin divine energies, we trace how these complementary forces work in our spiritual evolution. From this foundation, we explore how the Lord revolutionized human understanding of heaven, divine connection, and spiritual growth. This isn’t just historical analysis – it’s a living map for those seeking a deeper, more authentic spiritual path.

## These articles serve multiple purposes:

- Offering clear understanding of Wayist teaching
- Providing resources for teachers and devotees
- Supporting those seeking a new spiritual home
- Helping bridge different spiritual traditions
- Showing how ancient wisdom applies today

## For those who feel a heart connection with heaven but find themselves alienated from traditional religious structures, this collection offers:

- A fresh understanding of divine love
- Direct access to spiritual guidance
- Freedom from religious trauma
- Connection with divine family
- Path of authentic growth

## The Lord’s teaching, freed from centuries of religious overlay, reveals:

- Universal truth beyond religious boundaries

- Natural evolution of consciousness
